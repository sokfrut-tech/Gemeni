# Research Ledger Schema

Every research discovery that may influence the product should record:

- source URL/repository
- source language/region
- component or technique
- exact version/commit when available
- license and attribution requirements
- problem addressed
- evidence supporting the claim
- experiment branch/commit
- tests performed
- result
- knowledge state
- risks and known limitations
- whether the solution is suitable for ColorOS-first deployment

No external discovery becomes a factory default without reproducible evidence.
